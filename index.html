Order No. 001 — Our Story

A kitchen that keeps its receipts.

We started with one tawa, one grinding stone, and a stubborn belief that food tastes better when it's cooked for someone rather than for a queue. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is made after your order lands — not before.

From the first order slip to the last table cleared, we track every plate — so what reaches you is exactly what you asked for, at the temperature it deserves.
